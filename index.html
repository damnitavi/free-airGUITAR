Aerofret

Your webcam watches both hands. The left one chooses the chord shape, the right one strums it across six physically modelled strings with a real guitar body behind them.

  • Fretting hand slide left / right through the 12 roots · 1 finger = power chord, 2 = major, 3 = minor, 4 = 7th, four fingers with the thumb tucked = maj7, fist = palm mute
  • Strum hand swipe down or up through the air to strum · pinch thumb + index to pick a single string
  • Private by design hand tracking runs inside this page — no video leaves your machine

The instrument itself is hands-only — nothing above needs a keyboard. Mouse and keys still work too: ← → root · 1–5 chord type · space strums · P picks, and the neck strip, tuning chips and volume slider stay clickable.
